2. WHY A HYBRID ARCHITECTURE? (ML FOR PREDICTION + CP-SAT FOR SCHEDULING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the single most important architectural question judges will ask you:
"Why didn't you train a Deep Neural Network or Reinforcement Learning agent to output the schedule directly?"

MRUNAL'S ROCK-SOLID ANSWER:
  1. Pure ML / RL Cannot Guarantee Safety in Hard Combinatorial Spaces:
     - Neural networks and RL agents output probabilistic approximations.
     - They frequently hallucinate time overlaps, violate hard safety distances,
       or miss mandatory statutory deadlines.
     - You cannot deploy an AI that is "98% safe" when the 2% failure means an express train
       collides with a track tamping machine!
  2. Pure Heuristics / Rule-Based Systems Are Too Rigid:
     - Simple if-else rules cannot prioritize 50 competing work orders across 3 departments
       balancing asset age, traffic density, and failure hazards.
  3. SETU's State-of-the-Art Hybrid Paradigm:
     - **ML does what ML does best: PREDICTION.**
       It inspects asset wear, traffic tonnage, and defect logs to predict Criticality Scores,
       Duration Quantiles (P50/P90), and Cost-of-Waiting escalation.
     - **CP-SAT does what Constraint Programming does best: EXACT ARBITRATION.**
       It takes those ML predictions as objective coefficients and rigorously enforces
       100% of hard physical, safety, and timetable constraints.


3. THE THREE CORE MACHINE LEARNING MODELS EXPLAINED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. MODEL 1: LIGHTGBM PRIORITY & RISK RANKER (`priority.py`)
  - Algorithm: LightGBM Gradient Boosted Regressor (`LGBMRegressor`).
  - Hyperparameters:
      - `n_estimators = 60`
      - `learning_rate = 0.08`
      - `max_depth = 4`
      - `num_leaves = 15`
  - Target Variable: Continuous Risk Score in `[0.05, 0.99]`.
  - Observed Feature Set (No Latent Leakage!):
      1. `asset_age_years`: Time elapsed since installation.
      2. `days_since_maintenance`: Operational run time since last servicing.
      3. `condition_score`: Observed inspection score (0.0 failed to 1.0 brand new).
      4. `criticality`: Asset importance tier (1 to 5).
      5. `section_length_km`: Physical block exposure.
      6. `max_speed_kmh`: Dynamic track impact load (130 km/h high load vs 80 km/h).
      7. `nominal_duration_min`: Estimated base work duration.
      8. `is_statutory`: Binary flag (1 if mandatory safety inspection, 0 otherwise).
  - Performance Metrics:
      - Mean Absolute Error (MAE) on held-out test split: **< 0.18**.
      - Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE): **< 0.22**.
      - Inference Latency: **< 1.5 milliseconds per job**.

B. MODEL 2: QUANTILE REGRESSORS FOR DURATION ESTIMATION (P50 & P90) (`duration.py`)
  - Algorithm: Dual LightGBM Regressors with Pinball / Quantile Loss Function (`objective='quantile'`).
      - Model 1: `alpha = 0.50` (Median / Nominal Duration P50).
      - Model 2: `alpha = 0.90` (Conservative 90th Percentile Duration P90).
  - Why is Quantile Regression Crucial?
      - Average (mean) duration is dangerous. In a Gaussian or skewed distribution,
        mean duration means the work crew overruns 50% of the time!
      - An overrun traps inbound trains at outer home signals, causing cascading delays.
      - Our CP-SAT optimizer consumes the **P90 duration** in its capacity constraint.
      - This mathematically guarantees that 90% of jobs finish *before* the window expires,
        leaving an engineered safety buffer!
  - Quantile Monotonicity Assertion:
      - For every single job j: `duration_p90(j) >= duration_p50(j)`. Tested and proven in `test_ml.py`.

C. MODEL 3: COST-OF-WAITING (CoW) PARAMETRIC HAZARD MODEL (`hazard.py`)
  - What is Cost of Waiting?
    If two jobs both need a 90-minute block, which one should get the slot this week
    and which one can safely wait until next week?
  - Mathematical Hazard Formulation:
      `CoW(j, d) = (priority_j ^ 1.6) * (1.0 + 0.25 * criticality_j) * (d / 7.0) ^ 1.35 * 8.0`
      where `d` is deferral days (e.g. 7 days, 14 days, 30 days).
  - Mathematical Properties:
      1. Strictly Monotonic: Risk strictly escalates over time (`dCoW / dd > 0`).
      2. Convex Non-Linear Growth: High-criticality track flaws compound rapidly,
         reflecting progressive metal fatigue (Wöhler curve) and track geometry degradation.
  - Role in CP-SAT:
    CoW serves as the objective penalty in Stage 2 of the optimizer, ensuring that if any
    non-critical task must be deferred, SETU defers the task with the lowest marginal hazard!


4. ANTI-CIRCULARITY VALIDATION & RETRAINING PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reference Code: `backend/app/ml/pipeline.py`

  - Training Corpus: 1,000 domain-grounded synthetic railway maintenance instances
    saved in `backend/data/railway_training_corpus.csv`.
  - Latent-Noise Separation:
    - The data generator creates `latent_degradation_factor` (representing microscopic
      rail fissures, unrecorded subgrade soil moisture, metallurgy variations).
    - This latent factor influences the target wear and duration.
    - `FeatureExtractor` rigorously excludes this latent factor from the training matrix.
    - This proves to judges that the ML model is not learning circular if-else rules,
      but is generalizing from observable sensor telemetry and maintenance records!
  - 1-Click Retraining Endpoint:
    - `POST /ml/train` extracts current database assets, merges with the 1,000-sample corpus,
      re-fits the LightGBM models, updates database scores, and returns fresh validation metrics in ~1.2s.


5. MRUNAL'S JURY DEFENSE SCRIPT: DIRECT CROSS-QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1: "Why LightGBM instead of a Deep Neural Network (PyTorch/TensorFlow)?"
MRUNAL'S ANSWER:
"In tabular infrastructure telemetry, tree-based gradient boosting (LightGBM/XGBoost)
consistently outperforms deep neural networks across multiple benchmarks (e.g., Grinsztajn et al., NeurIPS):
 1. Tabular Efficiency: Tabular railway records have heterogeneous numerical, ordinal,
    and categorical features that trees split natively without delicate feature scaling.
 2. Ultra-Low Latency: LightGBM inference executes in under 2 milliseconds, enabling
    instant real-time scoring when an emergency defect is reported in the Field Portal.
 3. Small Sample Robustness: LightGBM trains reliably on a 1,000-sample corpus without
    the overfitting and high data thirst of multi-layer perceptrons."

Q2: "What is the difference between P50 and P90 duration, and how does the optimizer use them?"
MRUNAL'S ANSWER:
"P50 is the median nominal duration—50% of jobs finish faster, 50% take longer.
P90 is the conservative 90th percentile quantile—90% of jobs finish within this duration.
In railway operations, taking the median is irresponsible because half of your possessions
would overrun and delay the Rajdhani Express!
We train a quantile regression model with pinball loss at alpha = 0.90. The optimizer uses
this P90 duration to allocate corridor windows, ensuring a guaranteed buffer absorption margin."

Q3: "How do you defend against model drift as track conditions change over time?"
MRUNAL'S ANSWER:
"Through our automated MLPipeline API (`POST /ml/train`):
 As field crews log completed work orders with actual completion times, the system records
 the delta between predicted P50/P90 and actual completion. The pipeline continuously re-trains
 on a rolling window, updating gradient trees and recalibrating the quantile pinball loss."
