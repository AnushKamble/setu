--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reference Document: `setu-audit.md` Section 6 & `backend/app/api/plans.py`

This was a key point emphasized in our architectural audit:
  - What is BDMS?
    BDMS (Block Demand and Management System) is Indian Railways' official portal for
    registering sanctioned corridor blocks.
  - SETU does NOT attempt to illegally replace BDMS.
  - SETU acts as the **Upstream Optimization Brain**:
    It takes raw requests, computes the optimal convoy schedule, and exports a fully
    formatted, compliant **Indian Railways BDMS Requisition Bundle**:
      - Requisition IDs and Section Identifiers.
      - Sanctioned Window Slots and Durations.
      - Departmental Permit-to-Work (PTW) OHE Isolation flags.
      - Speed Restriction Caution Orders (30–50 km/h).
      - Standard Protection Method per General Rule (GR) 15.09 (Flagmen & Detonators).
      - One-click cryptographic JSON/CSV export ready for immediate filing by the DOM.

Q2: "Why should Indian Railways buy or adopt this when they already have BDMS?"
SHARANYA'S ANSWER:

spot conflicts and guess when to bundle jobs across Engineering, TRD, and S&T.

mathematically proven schedule in seconds and exports the exact BDMS bundle for the officer
to sign off and upload with a single click. It enhances BDMS rather than replacing it."
