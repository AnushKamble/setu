1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ELEVATOR PITCH FOR JUDGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SETU is an upstream decision-support and possession-planning intelligence platform
designed for Indian Railways (IR). It bridges the historical departmental silos
between:
  1. Engineering (Track / P-Way) via Track Management System (TMS)
  2. Traction Distribution (OHE Electrical) via Traction Distribution Management System (TDMS)
  3. Signal & Telecom (S&T) via Signal Maintenance Management System (SMMS)

Today in Indian Railways, each department independently approaches the Section
Controller (SCOR) for track possession (corridor maintenance blocks). This manual,
decentralized booking creates:
  - Severe traffic bunching and express train detention (Rajdhani, Vande Bharat, etc.).
  - Fragmented, repeated track closures on the same physical section.
  - Idle track capacity where one department works while the adjacent line or
    electrical wire sits idle, followed hours later by another shutdown.

SETU replaces this fragmented workflow with a mathematically proven, AI-prioritized,
and multi-department coordinated block planning engine. By deploying Google OR-Tools
CP-SAT combinatorial optimization, LightGBM machine learning, and an independent
Zero-Trust Safety Validator, SETU:
  - Slashes total corridor possessions by 38.9% (from 18 manual down to 11 coordinated blocks).
  - Eliminates 810 minutes (13.5 hours) of unnecessary track closures weekly on the corridor.
  - Generates 7 multi-department "Joint Convoys", co-locating track, electrical, and signalling gangs.
  - Guarantees ZERO timetable collisions with passenger express services.
  - Achieves sub-second optimization (<0.3s execution time).


2. THE PROBLEM STATEMENT: DECONSTRUCTED & DIRECTLY MAPPED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reference Document: PS_TRACEABILITY.md

The official Smart India Hackathon problem statement requires five core pillars:

[PS-1] DATA INTEGRATION:
  - Requirement: Integration of maintenance demands (defects, overdue maintenance)
    from TMS, SMMS, and TDMS with corridor block availability as per Train Time Table
    and Goods Trains forecast (COA).
  - SETU Answer: Canonical Railway Ledger (`backend/app/models/railway.py`), Ingestion
    Adapters, anti-circularity latent-noise synthetic corpus generator, and the live
    Field Engineer Requisition Portal (`/portal`).

[PS-2] AI/ML PRIORITIZATION:
  - Requirement: Prioritize and schedule tasks based on criticality, urgency, and
    impact on asset availability.
  - SETU Answer: LightGBM Priority Ranker (outputting risk score in [0, 1]), Quantile
    Duration Regressor (P50 nominal duration and P90 conservative buffer), and
    Cost-of-Waiting ordinal hazard function (predicting risk escalation per 7-day deferral).

[PS-3] COMBINATORIAL OPTIMIZATION & CONVOYS:
  - Requirement: Maximize asset uptime by minimizing downtime and efficiently
    coordinating multi-department activities.
  - SETU Answer: Google OR-Tools CP-SAT discrete solver using boolean convoy decision
    variables (z_{j, j', w}), pairwise domain compatibility matrix safe(j, j'), and
    lexicographic multi-stage optimization.

[PS-4] MULTI-HORIZON CONSISTENCY:
  - Requirement: Provide block plans over multiple time horizons (weekly and monthly)
    to support short-term and long-term planning.
  - SETU Answer: Two-Tier Rolling Horizon Coordinator. The monthly strategic horizon
    sets a coarse possession-hour quota envelope per section (e.g. <= 45h/month);
    the weekly tactical horizon optimizes exact 5-minute slots strictly bounded by
    the monthly envelope. (Corrected strategic-to-tactical dependency direction).

[PS-5] SAFETY, DYNAMIC REPLANNING & DATA-DRIVEN REPLACEMENT:
  - Requirement: Ensure uninterrupted train operations, replace manual guesswork, and
    provide dynamic replanning under live disruptions.
  - SETU Answer: Zero-Trust Independent Safety Validator (7 decoupled checks),
    Dynamic Replanner with Frozen State (<0.05s warm-start re-solve), and the
    interactive What-If Corridor Disruption Simulator.

6. TOP 5 JURY TRAP QUESTIONS & UNIVERSAL DEFENSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: "Is SETU replacing the Section Controller (SCOR) or the Railway Board's BDMS?"
  - DEFENSE: "No. SETU is an UPSTREAM decision-support tool. It produces an optimized,
    conflict-free, multi-department proposal bundle for the Divisional Operations
    Manager (DOM) to review, pin, and formally file into BDMS. Humans remain in the
    loop (HITL) at all times."

Q2: "Why didn't you use Reinforcement Learning or an LLM for scheduling?"
  - DEFENSE: "In railway safety, non-deterministic models like LLMs or RL cannot prove
    safety bounds and hallucinate time slots. CP-SAT is a deterministic constraint
    programming solver that mathematically proves global optimality and guarantees
    zero hard safety constraint violations."

Q3: "How do you know synthetic data isn't biased or circular with your ML model?"
  - DEFENSE: "We engineered an Anti-Circularity Latent-Noise Generator. It injects
    unobserved latent variables (metallurgy batch anomalies, ballast moisture) that
    influence asset degradation but are strictly HIDDEN from the ML model features,
    preventing circular data leakage."

Q4: "What happens if CP-SAT finds NO feasible schedule?"
  - DEFENSE: "SETU never crashes or outputs a blank screen. Our automated Infeasibility
    Relaxation Engine steps in: it relaxes the lowest-penalty soft constraint (e.g.
    sliding statutory deadlines by 24 hours), suggests alternative windows, and raises
    an urgent priority escalation flag for the human controller."

Q5: "Why P90 duration instead of average duration?"
  - DEFENSE: "In railway track work, duration overruns cause cascading train delays.
    Scheduling with P50 (mean/median) means 50% of jobs overrun. Scheduling with P90
    provides a conservative engineering buffer so following trains never get caught
    behind a lingering maintenance crew."
