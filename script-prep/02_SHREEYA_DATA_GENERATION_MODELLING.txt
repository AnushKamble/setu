6. THE ANTI-CIRCULARITY MECHANISM: A CRITICAL TECHNICAL MOAT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reference Document: `00-SIX-DAY-MASTER-PLAN.md` Part 1 #14 & `DECISIONS.md` Decision 004

This is one of the most sophisticated technical points in SETU. Memorize this thoroughly:

THE FLAW IN ORDINARY HACKATHON PROJECTS (The "Circular Synthetic Trap"):
  - Most student teams write synthetic data rules like:
      `if age > 10 years: priority = 0.9`
  - Then they train a Machine Learning model on that data.
  - The ML model easily achieves 99% accuracy because it is simply learning the exact
    deterministic Python if-else code the students wrote 10 minutes earlier!
  - Judges will call this out as: "Circular, artificial, and trivial."

HOW SETU FIXES THIS (Anti-Circularity Latent-Noise Generator):
  - In the real world, rail steel degradation is influenced by unobserved physics:
    sub-surface rail inclusions, local micro-climatic humidity, unrecorded ballast moisture,
    and rolling contact fatigue batch variance.
  - In `backend/app/datagen/generator.py`, SETU generates **Latent Unobserved Factors**
    (`latent_degradation_factor` in `Asset`).
  - These latent factors drive the TRUE degradation and failure time.
  - BUT THESE LATENT FACTORS ARE STRICTLY HIDDEN AND OMITTED from the feature matrix
    given to the LightGBM ML model!
  - The ML model only observes surface features: age, gross tonnage (GMT), section speed,
    and visual inspection history.
  - Therefore, the ML model must genuinely generalize in the presence of noise and
    cannot overfit to synthetic rules. This gives SETU true scientific credibility!

Q2: "Why do some jobs take 150 minutes while others take 90 minutes?"
SHREEYA'S ANSWER:
"In real operations, maintenance duration is heterogeneous:
 - Track tamping requires mobilizing a heavy machine from a loop line, stabilizing the track,
   and packing sleepers over a 2 km stretch, taking 180–240 minutes.
 - An S&T point machine overhaul is localized to a single switch motor at a station yard,
   taking 60–90 minutes.
 - In our model, we estimate both P50 (nominal execution time) and P90 (conservative
   90th percentile buffer) to account for field mobilization overheads."

Q3: "How do you model freight traffic when freight trains don't have strict timetables like Rajdhani?"
SHREEYA'S ANSWER:
"That is modeled after Indian Railways' COA (Control Office Application) freight forecasting.
We model two categories: timetabled container trains (CONCOR rakes) with assigned corridor slots,
and demand-driven bulk freight (coal/fertilizer rakes) with flexible dispatch windows.
This allows our CP-SAT optimizer to explore opportunistic maintenance windows between freight
paths without delaying passenger express traffic."
