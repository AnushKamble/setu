       restrained border contrasts (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).

C. SPACE-TIME MAREY STRING CHART (`MareyChartView.jsx`):
  - What is a Marey Chart?
    In Indian Railways, Section Controllers (SCOR) do not use Gantt charts. They use
    Marey diagrams (Time on X-axis, Kilometer distance on Y-axis). Trains are diagonal
    strings whose slope represents speed.
  - SETU Implementation:
    - Rendered in native SVG with pan, zoom, and live scrubbing.
    - Shaded rectangular horizontal bands represent track possessions.
    - Demonstrates ZERO line intersections: Passenger train strings pass cleanly
      around shaded possession windows without crossing into active track closures.
    - Includes 15-minute "Safety Auras" around express train paths.

5. VISHAL'S JURY DEFENSE SCRIPT: DIRECT CROSS-QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1: "Why do you have both a Gantt Chart and a Marey String Chart? Isn't that redundant?"
VISHAL'S ANSWER:
"They serve two completely different operational personas in Indian Railways:
 1. The Possession Gantt is used by the Senior Divisional Engineer (Sr. DEN) and
    department heads to plan gang mobilization, crew rosters, and equipment allocation over time.
 2. The Marey String Chart (Distance-Time Graph) is the official operational standard
    of the Section Controller (SCOR). It proves train path clearance. A controller cannot
    verify safety from a Gantt chart alone because they must see train trajectories, speeds,
    and headway clearances across kilometer space. SETU provides both views fully synchronized."

Q2: "What happens if a field engineer submits a new emergency defect while the planner is looking at the screen?"
VISHAL'S ANSWER:
"The system is architected reactively:
 1. When an SSE logs an emergency in our Field Requisition Portal, the backend updates the canonical ledger and CP-SAT re-solves.
 2. The frontend triggers an automated state refresh via onPlanUpdated callbacks.
 3. The planner automatically updates: the new convoy appears, the KPI delta updates,
    and a visual pulse highlight guides the controller's eye directly to the modified block."

Q3: "How does the UI prevent human error during manual block overrides?"
VISHAL'S ANSWER:
"Through our Human-in-the-Loop guardrails in BlockDrawer:
 When an operator attempts to reassign a block to an alternative window, SETU does not allow
 arbitrary free typing of timestamps. It queries `/api/plans/candidate-windows`, filtering
 only legally permitted corridor windows on that track section that have already passed
 duration and curfew constraints. If an operator overrides a block into a risky window,
 the Zero-Trust Safety Validator immediately flags the violation in red before BDMS signoff."
