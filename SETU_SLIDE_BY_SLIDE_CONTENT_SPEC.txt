--- BLOCK 2.1: THE CURRENT BOTTLENECK (TOP-LEFT CARD) ---
* Header Badge: TODAY'S REALITY: DEPARTMENT SILOS
* The Real Problem in Plain Terms:
  * Track Team (P-Way): Requests a morning track closure (09:00 - 12:00) to repair rails.
  * Electrical Team (TRD): Requests an afternoon closure (13:30 - 15:30) to inspect overhead wires.
  * Signal Team (S&T): Requests an evening closure (16:30 - 18:00) to service track switches.
* The Bottleneck:
  * Section Controllers manage these requests manually on paper forms and phone calls. Tracks are shut down 3 separate times (390 minutes total lost). Freight trains are held at outer signals, causing ripple delays across the network.

--- BLOCK 2.2: THE SETU SOLUTION (TOP-RIGHT CARD) ---
* Header Badge: THE SETU REVOLUTION: SYNCHRONIZED WORK WINDOWS
* Subtitle: ONE COMBINED TRACK WINDOW: 11:00 - 14:30 (210 min active work)
* Coordinated Execution:
  * Track Team: Tamping machine works on track from Km 102.0 to 104.5.
  * Electrical Team: Works on overhead wires between Km 102.5 and 104.0 with power safely cut.
  * Signal Team: Inspects rail switches and signals at Km 104.2 during the same window.
* Breakthrough Result:
  * 1 track closure instead of 3. Returns 180 minutes of track availability back to passenger express and freight trains. Zero train cancellations.

--- BLOCK 2.3: PILLAR 1 — SHARED DIGITAL REQUEST HUB (BOTTOM-LEFT CARD) ---
* Header Badge: 1. ONE SHARED DIGITAL HUB
* Title: Unified Multi-Department Queue
* Key Points:
  * Brings maintenance requests from Track, Electrical, and Signal teams into one shared digital system.
  * Replaces scattered paper forms and phone calls with one clear view of all upcoming work.
  * Automatically scores which repairs are urgent based on track age and safety inspection records.

--- BLOCK 2.4: PILLAR 2 — SMART WORK BUNDLING (BOTTOM-CENTER CARD) ---
* Header Badge: 2. SMART WORK BUNDLING
* Title: Coordinated Multi-Job Scheduling
* Key Points:
  * Our computer engine groups compatible repair jobs in the same track section to run together.
  * Automatically enforces 500-meter safety separation between heavy machinery and ground crews.
  * Guarantees overhead high-voltage power is switched off before any work begins underneath.

--- BLOCK 2.5: PILLAR 3 — TIMETABLE GAP MATCHING (BOTTOM-RIGHT CARD) ---
* Header Badge: 3. TIMETABLE GAP MATCHING
* Title: Protecting Train Punctuality

  * Fits maintenance windows directly into natural time gaps between scheduled trains.
  * Protects high-speed passenger trains like Rajdhani & Vande Bharat from unexpected stops.
  * Keeps freight rakes moving steadily across high-traffic corridors without congestion pile-ups.
